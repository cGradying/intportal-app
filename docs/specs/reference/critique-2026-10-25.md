# Critique: IntPortal as shipped (2026-10-25, origin/main 4016d4c)

Step 1 of [PLAN-dive-environment](../PLAN-dive-environment.md). Evidence is the simulator pass on iPhone 18 Pro (iOS 27.0, demo data, 01:25–01:44 local) in the Day room (System, light) and the Night room (System, dark). Screenshots are local only, in `docs/review/dive-env/NN-*.png`. Code facts are in the plan's audit appendix.

## Scores (0 = broken, 4 = ship-proud)

| Lens | Score | One line |
|---|---|---|
| UX (never miss a deadline, glance, two taps) | 3 | Today answers "what's next and what's due" in one look. The week view and the sign-in gate weaken it. |
| UI engineering (tokens, rhythm, consistency) | 2 | Rooms are tokenised. Night surfaces, the chat and the hub are hardcoded, and three corner languages compete. |
| Motion | 1 | The dive is the best moment, then a hard swap to a cream page with a brightness flash. Today has no entrance. |
| Accessibility | 2 | Text sizes scale, but Reduce Motion leaks infinite loops, and some pixel text and dimmed controls are low contrast. |
| Environment and brand | 2 | The landing has a world: void, portal, LAR. The app does not: generic cards on a dot grid, with a different "night". |

## What already works (keep)
- **Up next card** (`03`, `13`). The campus scene over a giant `7:30AM` with "in 5h 48m" is the app's best glance. The time is the hero, and the scene is mood, not data.
- **Deadlines on Today** (`03`). Overdue / Today / Wed in colour on the left, name and course on the right. Calm and exact.
- **Free gaps** (`04`). Dotted "1h 30m free" bands between classes are honest and useful.
- **Grade stamps and "Personal best"** (`07`, `16`). The one place where pixel craft already wraps data well.
- **LAR chat stage** (`17`). The gradient sky with the dither edge reads as premium pixel. It is the environment the rest of the app should share.

## Findings, ranked

### P0 — breaks the brief
1. **Sign-in ends in a flashbang.** The landing is a near-black void (`01`, `02`). Step through dives, then `onenter()` swaps hard to Today (`+page.svelte:91-93`). In the System room on a light phone that is a cream page (`03`). `main` then runs `arrive` from `brightness(2.2) saturate(0.4)` for 0.65s (`+page.svelte:170-188`). Near-black, then an over-bright cream page, inside 2s. The plan's dive and dither replace this. The dive must end in the room the student lands in, not in the void.

### P1 — hurts every day
2. **Today has no entrance.** All cards are present on the first frame. Nothing leads the eye to Up next. The plan's 40 ms stagger fixes this.
3. **Two different nights.** The landing, hub and chat use the void (`#0b0710`, `#140c16`, `#170b1a`, all untokenised). The Night room is a neutral charcoal (`12`–`16`). Coming home from the chat (`17`) to Grades (`16`) changes the world's colour. One night family, tokenised.
4. **Reduce Motion leaks loops.** `app.css:181-188` sets durations to 0.01ms but not the iteration count. Scene, Jeepney and Gallery loops keep cycling and can flicker.
5. **LAR's hands teleport.** GEST offsets skip `rig()` easing in `Lar.svelte`, and the wave starts at full amplitude. Already scheduled as `fix/lar-hands`.
6. **The week view hides empty days.** With classes on Mon and Sun only, the week is two columns (`05`, `15`). It does not read as a week, and spatial memory breaks when a class moves. Blocks show only a split course code (`COMP` / `20073`), with no title and no room. There is no "moved" mark at all.

### P2 — polish that reads as "generic"
7. **Three corner languages.** Rounded 12px cards in Day (`03`), stepped pixel corners on night cards and the GPA card (`13`, `16`), and 5–6px rounded week blocks (`05`). Pick one per role: pixel notch for hero and stage surfaces, quiet radius or square for dense lists.
8. **The cube orb sits on content at rest.** It covers the grade stamp (`07`, `16`), the Ask LAR button (`09`) and the last week block (`15`). The page pads 150px at the bottom (`+page.svelte:174`), but the resting position overlaps. Give it a lane, or let it dodge and shrink on scroll.
9. **System-blue checkboxes.** No `accent-color` anywhere. "Hide vacant classes", Haptics and the calendar switches render iOS blue (`11`, `12`, `14`). One line of CSS, or pixel checkboxes.
10. **Montserrat does everything.** It sets display, labels, the hero time and the week-block codes, in 17 sizes (appendix). h2 equals body at the largest text size. `7:30AM` sets the meridiem as large as the digits. The hero time wants tabular pixel numerals with a small-caps AM.
11. **Step-through gate.** LAR's bubble overlaps the portal and runs off the right edge (`02`). The dimmed caption pill and the `PUP SIS · via sis8` line are low contrast.
12. **The sign-in form leaves in one frame.** In the recording, the dock vanishes between two frames. The 0.25s fade is too short to register. Exit choreography belongs to the dive spec.

### P3 — small
13. `0days`, `0cards` on Study (`08`): the unit has no gap, and zero states have no LAR line.
14. The You header puts the two mascots on top of the first card's edge (`09`).
15. A tab label cap of 13px (`TabBar.svelte:50`), against 20px for the same size elsewhere.

## What the market scan changes
See [market-scan-2026-10-25.md](market-scan-2026-10-25.md). It confirms three of the plan's calls and adds two.
- **Confirms:** two-layer type, playful chrome around calm data, and celebrations kept rare.
- **Adds:** grey out finished classes (as Things does), so the now-line becomes a state change. And give moved or cancelled items a label plus a pattern, not colour alone.
- **Balatro's feel set** (count-up with rising pitch, step-forward stagger, shake tiers, idle sway and a living background) is all pure feel. The compulsion parts are the hidden preview, slot suspense and run loops. The plan already drops them.
