The motion rules behind it

Pixel things step, interface things ease. Hops, stamps and fills use steps(). Pages, sheets and the cube's flight use the one ease, with a spring only where something lands.

Direction carries meaning. Pages slide 12 px toward the tab you picked, so you feel where you went. The chat rises from where the cube lives, and goes back the same way.

Count from what you last saw. The GPA counts from the value on your last visit, not from zero, and stamps thunk only when a grade is new to this phone.

The field answers, rarely. The dot grid ripples only when something lands: the end of the dive, a sync, a done. Then it's dots again.

Things grow from where they live. A class sheet opens out of its block, the chat out of the cube. Closing goes back the same way.

Then still. Nothing loops on content. The cube bobs, and LAR breathes in his chat; cards never move on their own. Reduce Motion skips all of it to the end state.

045 C13–C19

A "done" you want to do again

Pressing stays subtle; finishing is generous, and a run of them builds. Tap a deadline: the box fills in three steps, a line strikes through, sparks pop and the dot grid ripples, and a DONE stamp lands. A pixel bar under the title fills one step. The card lifts, tilts and drops into Done, landing with a squash and a soft tick. Mark the next within 4 s and it rings higher, shakes harder and shows its count. Clear the list and the bar flashes, LAR hops with a thumbs up, and tile confetti bursts three times. Then the screen is still.

What makes it want another tap

Rising pitch. Each done within 4 s of the last climbs the pentatonic scale: C, D, E, G, A, then up an octave. It can't sound wrong, and the climb rewards a run of real work. It resets after a pause. Balatro raises the pitch on every scored card the same way.

Short and layered. A 2 ms click on press, a soft pluck for the check, a fifth above for the strike, and a low thump under the stamp. Each note is detuned a few cents at random, so the hundredth tap doesn't sound like the first.

Tiers, not slot machines. The first done nudges. The second and third in a run shake and show ×2, ×3. From the fourth on they shake harder and add a high sparkle. Nothing is random but the detune, and nothing is hidden.

Then silence. Each done finishes within 1.1 s and nothing loops. The list is still the moment the card lands.

In the app, sound starts off, with a switch in You (045 C19). It's on here so you can hear it. Haptics pair with each step: a tick, a success buzz, then a soft impact under the stamp.
