The chat: a clear header, LAR at work, suggestions that follow you

The header from the old sheet is back, inside the glass: LAR on his perch, his name, what he is doing right now, New chat and Close. It folds to a slim bar when the keyboard is up or you scroll.

While he works you see his real steps, nothing made up: what he read, what he searched, what he found and the line it matched, then Writing. When he answers, the trail folds into one line you can open again. The neuron grows one branch per step and reaches each note he found.

Suggestions sit in one row over the input. Before you ask, they come from your day; after an answer, from what it was about.
