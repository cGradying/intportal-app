Opening: Pour, and Pour with the dive's dither

You picked Pour. E and F mix it with the portal dive's pixel dither. E pours the same 27 atoms, trailing pixels, and knits their seams shut in pixels. F melts the cube into pixels that fill the window like a glass, its surface a settling dithered wave. Each turns the cube to face you first, moves in one continuous motion, and hands over to the real window in one frame.

Try it
