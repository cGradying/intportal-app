Opening: the cube changes its atom form

Tap the cube. Its 27 atoms turn to face you, lay themselves flat into a wall, and the wall grows into the chat; the seams melt into one pane of glass, then LAR comes out of his fire on his perch in the header. Closing plays it back into the cube. B is the other way I tried, for comparison.
