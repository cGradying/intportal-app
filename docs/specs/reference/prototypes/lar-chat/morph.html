Opening: the cube pours the window

Tap the cube. It turns to face you, then its 27 atoms pour upward out of that face in one wave, bottom rows first, and settle as the tiles of the chat: the top layer of the cube becomes the sky header, the rest the glass. The seams close, the sky lights up, and LAR comes out of his fire standing on its pixel edge. Closing pours it back into the cube.

Try it

About 0.66 s to open, 0.6 s to close. Slow motion runs it at a quarter speed. With Reduce Motion the window cross-fades and nothing moves.
