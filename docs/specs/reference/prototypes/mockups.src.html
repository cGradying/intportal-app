The original stays, in You

Why these four

Sharp system keeps your pick and pushes it harder: heavier titles, tighter tracking, and the pixel voice on every label, so the pixels read as part of the type system.

Squared uses Chakra Petch, whose cut corners echo the stepped card corners.

Soft blocks uses Rubik, whose rounded squares sit between pixels and the phone's own UI.

Pixel titles puts Pixelify on screen titles and course codes, with Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading. It's the most seamless of the four, but it breaks DESIGN.md's Pixel Voice rule for headings, so picking it rewrites that rule.

In every direction, times, rooms and due dates stay in the reading face.

045 C13–C19

A "done" you want to do again

Pressing stays subtle; finishing is generous. Tap a deadline: the box fills in three steps, a line strikes through the title, the card nudges, sparks pop from the box and a DONE stamp lands. Then the card slides down into Done, and the screen goes still. Mark several in a row and each one rings a step higher. Clearing the list plays a short run and LAR gives a thumbs-up.

Sound

What makes it want another tap

Rising pitch. Each done within 4 s of the last climbs the pentatonic scale: C, D, E, G, A, then up an octave. It can't sound wrong, and the climb rewards a run of real work. It resets after a pause. Balatro raises the pitch on every scored card the same way.

Short and layered. A 2 ms click on press, a soft pluck for the check, a fifth above for the strike, and a low thump under the stamp. Each note is detuned a few cents at random, so the hundredth tap doesn't sound like the first.

Then silence. Everything finishes within 0.8 s and nothing loops. The list is still the moment the card lands.

In the app, sound starts off, with a switch in You (045 C19). It's on here so you can hear it. Haptics pair with each step: a tick, a success buzz, then a soft impact under the stamp.
