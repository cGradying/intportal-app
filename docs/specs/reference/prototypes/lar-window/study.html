Study in parts, each one screen tall

Notes, Cards and Review get a short page each, one tap apart, instead of one long scroll. Three ways to move between them. Every phone is live: pull a notebook, open a note, review a card.

A: Tiles homeStudy opens on four live tiles. Each opens its own page, with Study as the way back.
B: Top switchNotes, Cards and Review across the top. The part slides in under the gold.
C: Thumb railSwipe between parts, or tap the rail beside the +. Everything you switch with sits in thumb reach.

Try it

Tap a part to open it. Start a review and tap the card to flip it: Got it takes a card off every count, in every part, and logs it under Done. Tap a deck with cards due to review only that deck. The + offers Ask LAR, a new note and a new deck, as it does today.
