Greeting: after the dive, the day says hello

The portal dive ends, then a slow hello, then Today, with no greeting of its own at the top. Tap anywhere to skip. Drag the bar to scrub; Replay plays it again.

Two picks here. Earlier you ruled that after the dive Today must not rise in again: it is simply there. So also pick how Today arrives: rising block by block as the hello lifts, or already in place as the hello fades off it. Switch between them with the Today button.
