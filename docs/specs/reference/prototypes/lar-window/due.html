Adding a due: it reads as you type

No Read it step and no form. It reads what you type, chips fix what it got wrong, and one Add sits under your thumb. The window grows out of Add and shrinks back into the Due card.

A: Command barOne line on the keyboard. What it read grows above it; tap any underlined part to fix it. Return adds. The keyboard never drops.
B: TicketWhat it read is a ticket you can rename. Every fix is one tap in the three rows under it: when, subject, kind.
C: Week stripThe day is picked on your own two weeks, with the subject's class days marked in its colour. Good for "next COMP class".

Try it

These drive all three phones. Each phone is live too: tap Add on its Due card, type, tap a chip, tap the grey keyboard to put it away, swipe the window down to cancel, or tap a due to edit it.
