Palette

Do / Don't

DO

  • Tint rim, studs, cube, belly frame and swirl; nothing else.
  • Scale by whole numbers: 2, 4, 8.
  • Pass from and since when the state changes, so it eases.
  • Keep the hands, antenna and cube as their own parts.

DON'T

  • Recolour the obsidian body for a portal.
  • Swap in the vector LAR; he is pixels.
  • Hard-cut between poses, or let a part leave the box.
  • Use a gold rim on gold eyes; the gold portals get magenta.
