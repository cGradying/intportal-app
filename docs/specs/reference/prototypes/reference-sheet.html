01 · Rules

Six rules the three sources agree on

  • TileTiles have gaps

    Flat squares, 11 on a 14 pitch. No texture, grain, bevel or shader inside the portal.

  • CalmOne gold

    Gold means "do this" or "you are here". A second action is ghost or underlined, never a second hue.

  • TileThe theme stays in the portal

    A portal's ramp colours its tiles and interior only. Never a card fill, a gradient or text.

  • CalmOne pixel moment per screen

    Clear first, charming second. No art in front of the next class, a grade or a due date.

  • BoardPixelify speaks for the other side

    LAR's tag, hints, stamps and the wordmark. Never a heading, a button or a sentence read twice.

  • BoardSteps for pixels, ease for the interface

    steps() and 30 fps for art; one ease for sheets, docks and fades.

04 · The tile portal

24 blocks, 96 tiles, one clock

Tile

A block is 2 × 2 tiles. A tile is 11 px on a 14 px pitch at 1×, and the gap is part of the look. The inner edge of the frame is one tone darker.

The pop, three stepped frames

Stage

Portal

Fit · the same renderer on three phone shapes

The portal takes whatever height the sign-in card leaves and picks the largest whole-pixel pitch that fits 12 × 16 tiles with a tile of margin, so it stays crisp on every aspect ratio.

05 · Pixel art library

The board's art, drawn the portal's way

Solid is how the art ships today. Tile draws every art pixel as a portal tile with its gap, which ties the mascots and icons to the landing. Tile mode suits large sizes (LAR on the landing, empty states); keep Solid for 18–24 px icons.

07 · Shape, depth, motion

Stepped corners, hairlines, two kinds of motion

Calm

Stepped corners cut with clip-path: 4 px on cards and sheets, 3 px on slabs. Never a radius on a pixel surface.

Calm

The gold lip: a 3 px shade at the bottom that flips to the top when pressed. Press it.

Calm
ease

Interface motion: one curve, cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1). Docks 0.7 s, sheets 0.35 s.

Board
steps

Pixel motion: steps(). The portal runs 30 fps while it moves, 15 fps at rest.

09 · From the board into the app

Pixel moments to bring in next

Proposals
  • Rubber stamps on marks

    Vacant, online and in person as stamps that thunk onto a class on the week grid, in Pixelify with an ink mask.

  • Dithered free time

    The gap between two classes on Today as a Bayer-dithered strip, quiet and on the ground colour.

  • Board flame for streaks

    The board's 7 × 9 flame, gold tip over red, beside the study streak count.

  • Mini portal mark

    A 13 × 18 tile portal with a live swirl as the app's mark on You and the about sheet.

    Topics as isometric tiles, lit by mastery, from the board's quiz screen. A Study tab moment.
