Student IntPortal · landing v9

The portal is the dot grid, lit. Same tiles, same renderer (crates/intportal-tiles, embedded here as WebAssembly), now standing on the app's own night ground. Tap the portal, the plaque and the buttons; the states below switch the stage.

What changed from the shipped landing

  • One ground. The landing stands on the app's night ground. Its dots sit on the portal's tile pitch, so the dive starts and ends in the same world.
  • The portal lights the ground. Dots near the frame grow and take the portal's colour, falling off over four tiles. Flat squares, no blur, no bevel.
  • A floor without a floor. The frame and mouth mirror below in tiles that fade over four rows.
  • LAR has a place. He stands at the portal's right foot. His line sits beside him and never covers a tile.
  • The card is shorter. Birthdate and password share a row, which gives the portal about 64 pt more.
  • The plaque replaces "1 of 108" and the arrow glyphs. It names the school and says whether its portal is answering.

What a student can do before signing in

  • Peek inside: Today, Schedule, Grades and Study on sample data.
  • Look around the whole app on sample data, in release builds too.
  • See if SIS is up, and tap the chip to check again.
  • Choose a school from the wall, or swipe the orbit.
  • Touch the portal: a ring runs out from the finger.
  • Read how the login is kept, or open the SIS password reset.
