LARLakbay-Aral Robot, from the other side of the portal

Role
The voice. Coach, tips, empty states, IntAssis. Isko is the student; LAR talks.
Build
An obsidian pebble with block ears, a dark visor with gold eyes, a belly window onto the current portal, a gold spark antenna, two floating mitts and a tiny orbiting cube.
Change
Same character, now drawn as smooth shapes with no outline. One geometry (src/lib/lar/vector.ts) renders as SVG or into a canvas at any resolution.

04 · Palette and build

Obsidian, gold, and the portal in his belly

  • Bob3.1 rad/s, 2.6 units. The mitts lag 0.7 rad behind and drift 3 units.
  • AntennaSways with the bob; the spark pulses ±12 %.
  • CubeOrbits at 2.2 rad/s, passing behind him when it is on the far side.
  • BlinkAbout a tenth of a second every 2.9–3.7 s, when open.
  • Reduce MotionOne still pose; nothing bobs, blinks or orbits.

05 · Resolution test bench

How he arrives on the landing

Each case runs the app's real tile portal. LAR comes out of the swirl, then floats round the portal, passing behind it. Pick the one that feels right and tell me the letter.

APixel resolve

He comes through as a coarse low-res sprite and snaps up in steps (16 → 32 → 64 → full) until he is crisp. Echoes the tiles.

BDepth resolve

Resolution, a soft blur and light follow his depth. Behind the portal or far away he is low-res and dim; in front of it he is sharp.

CAlways crisp

Always full resolution. Depth reads through scale, shadow and drawing order only. The calmest option.

06 · Rules

Keep him LAR

  • DoShow the current portal's ramp in his belly window. It's the one place the theme touches him.
  • DoKeep gold for his eyes, ear tips, spark and cube only.
  • DoLet him face where he is going (turn) and react with mood, never with a second voice.
  • Don'tDraw an outline or pixel border around him.
  • Don'tPut text, logos or a school's colours on his body.
  • Don'tPark him over the sign-in card or a field the student is using.
