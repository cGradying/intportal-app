The pixel LAR, in each university's colours: his body runs as a gradient from the ramp's deep hue to the accent's, a sheen sweeps across him now and then, a soft glow sits behind him, and he spawns out of a fire in his portal's ramp. Drawn by the app's own drawLar() and rig().

Do / Don't

DO

  • Dress him in the university: body gradient, outline, rim, studs, cube, swirl, fire.
  • Keep the gold eyes and spark in every portal; that is how you know him.
  • Scale by whole numbers: 2, 3, 4, 8.
  • Switch states through rig(), so every change eases.

DON'T

  • Swap in the vector LAR; he is pixels.
  • Hard-cut between poses, or let a part leave the box.
  • Smooth the scale-up; the gradients are dithered pixels, the glow is the one soft thing.
  • Animate under Reduce Motion; hold the still pose.
